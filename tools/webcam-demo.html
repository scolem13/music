How it works

Press Start and allow your camera and microphone. A note name appears and you click it on the keyboard. The recording shows the activity, your mouse or finger, each click, and your webcam in the corner.

Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded. When you stop, the video is saved to your downloads so you can email it to your teacher.
