The systems

SystemHow it fingers
Home positionThe thumb stays on the home note (the tune's lowest), one finger a step: C D E F G = 1 2 3 4 5. To reach A, 5 takes A and 4 shifts up to G, only in the run of notes that goes up there; elsewhere G is 5 again (Twinkle: 11 44 55 4 | 44 33 22 1 | 55 44 33 2). 4 shifting between F and G is a finger shift the paper's model does not allow, so the rules score it "not playable". Wider tunes are cut into pieces that fit, the hand moved to each piece's lowest note.
Scale fingersEach note gets its finger in the right-hand major scale of the key (C: C D E F G A B = 1 2 3 1 2 3 4; Twinkle: 1 1 2 2 3 3 2 | 1 1 3 3 2 2 1). The scale table is written from memory and not yet checked against a scale book.
Five-finger positionsThe page's first rule: five staff steps a position, the thumb on the lowest, a new position when the next note does not fit.
Least effortThe fingering with the fewest points under the rules below.

Rules

Points are added for each fingering; the lowest total wins. Weight 0 turns a rule off. Rules 1–12 and their points are the paper's (Music Perception 14.4, pp. 349–360). Rules marked site are not in the paper.

100% Scales every span in the table below. The paper's table is for an adult pianist's hand; children's hands are smaller.
