- Full scores (orchestra / band): MusicXML → ABC by `node scripts/full-score/convert.mjs score.mxl [out.abc]` (xml2abc, tidied for abcjs: one L:, each voice on one line, octave clefs in transposing voices as `octave=±1`), then checked against the MusicXML note by note (`score-check.cjs`: bar counts, bar lengths, bar alignment across voices, pitches and lengths, ranges, duplicate names, hidden notes, tempo changes; strategies in its header). `node scripts/full-score/arrange.mjs in.abc plan.json out.abc score.mxl` reorders / drops / shares staves (plan format in its header; cello + bass: drawn on one staff, played apart, since abcjs ignores a staff's second voice's transpose=) and rechecks. Player `apps/shared/full-score.js` (global FullScore: views by family / tick box / saved, play from a clicked note or a typed bar number (go to bar), Space = play / pause, follow; first screen drawn at once and the rest a system at a time in the background (`score.flush()`, tests wait for `data-drawing` to clear; the long line is drawn in pieces at one set of staff heights); pages side by side all one height (Page height control); no title in the drawn ABC (the page has it), the top staff at one height on every page ("Space above the top staff", grown to fit the tallest tempo / words / high notes); Staff spacing "Even" (one gap, the same on every page) or "Keep apart" (skyline); abcjs staffwidth is in screen px when scaled, and it numbers voice names by staff, not voice; time signatures and opening repeats lined up across key signatures; joining bar lines are each staff stroke's own width (a repeat's thin and thick lines, not its dots); its own systems in every layout (pages down, pages side by side with page turns, one long line with sticky names; one bar width for all; cutaway leaves out resting staves); staves placed by the page after abcjs draws (exact gap, or MuseScore-style skyline spacing), bar lines through families / the whole score, brackets stretched; bar numbers drawn by the page, centred over the bar line that starts the bar (abcjs's own count a pickup as 1); rests centred on two-voice staves; "Even out the loudness" (written dynamics, instruments at once, compressor; `AbcPlay` `opts.shape`); plays on the band samples via `AbcPlay` `tracks`; repeats unrolled by the player, not abcjs ("Skip repeats" = each bar once, last ending), one bar timeline for the shaded playing bar, Follow, Go to bar, clicked notes and Pause; stems bar by bar on two-voice staves (rules in its header, `[I:stem up|down|auto]` added to our abcjs copy); analysed library pieces open with `?analysed=<id>`, any score at `&bar=N`). Site page `theory/analysed-scores/full-scores.qmd` (list; `?id=` opens one, the list folded and the score first; `?tune=<%%id>` draws a Tune Library tune as a one-staff lead sheet played by the band (FullScore `o.band`: a Band style menu as on the Tunes page, AbcPlay `opts.abc`; the score's bar cursor re-set at each bar the band sounds via AbcPlay `opts.onBar`; starts on a bar), linked as "𝄞 score" from Real Book rows whose title matches a library tune in the Tune Directory sheet; scores in `apps/shared/data/full-scores/`: `index.json` + `<id>.abc` (the arranged ABC) + `<id>.notes.html`; bump `V` in the page after changing them). `node scripts/full-score/artifact.mjs` builds a self-contained page (samples embedded), first one Mozart 40 iv (https://claude.ai/artifact/RB3mN3KqCLE49EhJ5VkZaG; earlier Sadko page https://claude.ai/artifact/CPA8BixuL2sU7ytNSMs2QZ). - Analysed Scores Library (`theory/analysed-scores/view.qmd`, `apps/shared/score-view.js`: one page, `?id=` draws a piece through `AnalysedScores.add`; no id = searchable list): When in Rome's Bach chorales and Beethoven / Mozart sonata movements (Sapp kern) and WTC I preludes and OpenScore Lieder (When in Rome's score.mxl, words left out; voice staff named "Voice"), each whose score fits its analysis, as data in `apps/shared/data/scores/` (`index.json` + one file per collection, fetched when opened; bump `V` in score-view.js after a rebuild). Built by `node scripts/when-in-rome/bundle.mjs [set]`: lists every piece left out and why (no score, labels off the bars, fit < 0.8 = the analysis's chords not sounding, bar check `bar-check.cjs`). Mozart files are matched by Köchel number (Sapp's numbers differ after No. 14). An analysis a bar off its score (pickup numbered differently) is shifted a bar when that makes it fit. Clef changes inside a staff are written `[K:clef=…]` (before a tuplet, never inside). An analysis whose endings (m90a, m90b) or repeats are numbered differently from the score is re-numbered by `realign()` in bundle.mjs (per-bar shifts -3..+3 chosen by dynamic programming on the same fit test, jumps of at most 2, at most 6 changes; 9 pieces added 2026-11-09). Still left out: cadenza / unmetered bars (Beethoven 3-1), analyses that really do not fit (fit < 0.8 after realigning), labels on beats with no note (not a numbering problem), no analysis, no score. Tests `node tests/works/score-bundles.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail), `tests/works/score-view.cdp.js`
