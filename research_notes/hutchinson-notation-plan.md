# Notation for the Hutchinson excerpts: plan

Clip list: `research_notes/hutchinson-clips.org`, generated by `node scripts/works/hutchinson-clips.mjs`.
As of 2026-11-06 it holds 244 clips from 222 pieces.
- 7 clips have bar numbers, taken from the page text.
- 18 clips are already notated on the site: 14 are tunes and 4 are Classical Themes. A theme is only the opening, so it may not cover the clip.
- About 65 pieces look public domain (classical composers, traditional tunes). About 157 look in copyright (pop, film, jazz standards).
  This split was made by matching composer names. Check it piece by piece before using it.

## Goal per clip
- ABC for the bars the clip plays, and only those bars, bar-checked by script (rule 1).
- Bar numbers for the clip, from the score's own numbering.
- A timing map, so the clip can light its bars (RecSync, as for the full scores).

## Tiers, cheapest first

1. **Already in the site's data: just cut and number.** Pieces already in the When in Rome / Sapp / OpenScore bundles
   (`apps/shared/data/scores/`) or the full scores, such as the Bach chorales, WTC I, and the Mozart and Beethoven sonata movements.
   A script finds the clip's bars in the existing ABC and extracts them. Bar numbers come free.
   Cost: a script and a listening check per clip. Do the Bach Prelude in C, Mozart K. 545 and the Moonlight and Pathétique sonatas first.
2. **Public domain, with a machine-readable score elsewhere.** Sources: Mutopia (LilyPond), OpenScore / MuseScore (MusicXML),
   CPDL (choral), kern collections, and the Bach Inventions and Beethoven symphonies in MusicXML.
   Pipeline: `scripts/full-score/convert.mjs`, then `score-check.cjs`, then cut to the clip.
   Cost: finding each source, plus the converter's checks. Licences vary (most are CC or PD); record each one in `scripts/works-cited.py`.
3. **Public domain, scans only (IMSLP).** Run OMR (Audiveris, free) to MusicXML, then the tier 2 pipeline.
   OMR output needs a check against the scan note by note, so this costs the most of the PD tiers.
   Short clips can be typed straight into ABC instead.
4. **In copyright (the pop, film and jazz clips): teacher decision.** Writing out their melodies copies the composition.
   - Options:
     - (a) Store only the chord progression (Roman numerals plus bar lengths) and the form, which is not the protected melody.
       Time them to the clip in Timestamp Notes.
     - (b) Short melody excerpts for class use only, behind the site password, as fair-use quotation. Ask before doing this.
     - (c) Link to licensed sheet music (e.g. Musicnotes) instead.
   - Recommendation: (a) for all of them, and (b) only for clips the teacher names.
   - Cost of (a): low. A progression per clip, checked by ear or against Billboard/SALAMI chords where those exist.

## Bar numbers without new notation
- Tiers 1-2 give them from the score.
- For tier 4, count bars from the clip's start using the timing-map tool. The site's convention is bar 1 = first full bar.

## Order of work
1. Tier 1 script: match clips to the score bundles, cut the bars, write `bars` and an ABC path back into the org file.
2. Tier 4(a) progressions for the clips already in the Billboard/SALAMI data. That chord data is already on the site.
3. Tier 2, piece by piece, starting with the clips used most: Star-Spangled Banner (PD), Beethoven 1, Bach Invention 1.
4. Tier 3 last.
