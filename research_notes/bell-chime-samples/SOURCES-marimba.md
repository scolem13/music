# Marimba samples (staging, 2026-11-28)

Goal: clean single-note marimba for the Ambient Generator, low register first. Nothing here is used by the site yet. Nothing is committed.

- `raw/marimba-iowa/` (30 chromatic-scale files, 96 kHz 24-bit stereo, about 800 MB), `raw/marimba-vcsl/` (30 wavs), `raw/marimba-vsco/` (9 wavs) hold the downloads as fetched.
- `trimmed-marimba/` holds 383 mono AAC files at 96 kbps, 16.9 MB in all (353 Iowa, 30 VCSL).
- Processing: mono mix, 48 kHz. Iowa: each 50 s file holds 12 chromatic notes about 4 s apart (e.g. `C2B2` = C2 to B2). Onsets were found from the 100 ms RMS (rise over 6 dB and within 14 dB of the file's peak), refined on a 10 ms envelope, and each note was cut from 5 ms before its onset to 30 ms before the next onset (cap 10 s). VCSL: cut 20 ms before the onset, ended where the 50 ms RMS drops below -62 dB or at 14 s. Fade: last 30% (at most 2 s) with a cos^2 curve. Peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, then AAC at 96 kbps. Iowa files end by the fade, not a natural decay (the next note has begun, so the tail is the note's own decay at about -45 dB). VCSL files were high-passed at 45 Hz first (see concerns).
- Pitch: FFT (zero-padded) of 0.1 to 1.6 s after the onset, strongest peak within 1.5 semitones of the expected note, parabolic interpolation. A file whose measured note differs from the expected one, or is more than 50 cents off, was rejected.
- Noise floor: energy above 5 kHz in the last 0.4 s of the cut note (before the fade), relative to the strongest 0.4 s window of the note, in dB (full spectrum). Iowa notes end in their own decay, so these numbers include the decaying body; they are an upper bound for hiss. Rejected: floor above -40 dB, or length under 0.6 s.
- Names: `marimba-<source>-<note>-<dyn>[-<mallet>].m4a`, `s` for sharp. Iowa names carry the mallet (`yarn`, `rubber`, `cord`).

## Sources in detail

- Iowa: performer Andrew Thierauf, recorded 20 March 2013 in the anechoic chamber, 5 feet from the instrument, Earthworks QTC40 into a Metric Halo 2882, 24-bit; technicians Daniel Frantz and Will Huff. Normal strokes with yarn, cord and rubber mallets at pp, mf and ff. Files fetched: C2B2, C3B3, C4B4 for every mallet and dynamic, plus C5B5 for yarn. Not fetched: C6 and up, rolls, dead strokes, the zip sets.
- VCSL: `Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Marimba/Marimba_hit_Outrigger_<note>_<soft|med|loud>_01.wav` (44.1 kHz stereo). Ten labelled notes, three dynamics each (soft = p, med = mf, loud = f). Author Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner. **The labels sound an octave higher than named** (label `F1` is F2 at 87 Hz, `C2` is C3, and so on), so files are named by the measured pitch. Mallet not stated ("Outrigger" is the mic position).
