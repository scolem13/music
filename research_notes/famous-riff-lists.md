# Lists of famous riffs, themes, licks, intros and grooves, by style (found 2026-11-04)

Sources to compile from. Found by web search; most pages were seen only as search snippets, not opened, so sizes and
top picks are as the snippets give them. **Poll** = readers / listeners voted; **editors** = a magazine's staff or invited
musicians; **lesson** = a teaching list (licks with tab or notation); **blog** = one writer's opinion. Pages that looked
like content farms are left out.
