# Classical Themes

- Classical Themes (`tools/classical-themes.qmd`, `apps/themes/themes-page.js`): the 2,067 themes of the Musical Theme Dataset (MTD,
  Barlow & Morgenstern newly engraved, CC BY 4.0; credited on the page and in `scripts/sources/classical-themes.py`). `python3
  scripts/convert-mtd.py` (needs `pip install abc-xml-converter`, MTD in ~/Downloads/MTD_1.0.0) writes `scripts/theme-sources/mtd/<id>.abc`
  (ids = EDM numbers, the old set's; titles from `../classical-music-themes/tsvs/themes.tsv`, whose own midi2abc ABC is no longer used),
  and for scores with more than one voice also `theme-sources/mtd-two-voice/<id>.abc` (the one-voice file says so). Then
  `node scripts/import-themes.mjs` writes `apps/themes/themes-data.js` (sort values; a minor key from the score, the title or a guess
  from the melody, `modeFrom`); a hand fix in `scripts/theme-fixes/<id>.abc` wins. Filters are the Tune Assessment's finder
  (`TuneLibrary.renderPicker` with `onResults`, composer as the tag); sort on up to three keys, Show N / All (cards drawn as they
  scroll near), columns, Play. Each theme's ABC has `Q:1/4=N`, its recording's tempo (`scripts/mtd-tempo.py`,
  from MTD's note alignment; Play uses it, not drawn), and `N:` its movement from MusicBrainz (`scripts/mtd-movements.py` ->
  `theme-sources/mtd-movements.json`; on the cards and in search). Catalogue numbers and years (composed, published, first performed): `python3 scripts/theme-works.py` (the old set's titles, then MusicBrainz work -> parent work -> Wikidata) -> `theme-sources/theme-works.json`, read by `import-themes.mjs` (themes it finds no year for: `python3 -I scripts/print-books/work-years.py --themes`
  -> `theme-sources/theme-years-wikidata.json`, composer + catalogue number on Wikidata, used only where theme-works.json has none) (both pages; on the cards, in search, two sorts). Play is the band's piano (`MusicAudio`), timed by abcjs's
  `setTiming` on the drawn score (beats of the meter: converted with `getBeatLength`) with the red note lit from the same
  schedule. Bar numbers: `python3 -I scripts/pdmx-match.py --all` finds each theme in PDMX full scores (`~/Downloads/PDMX.csv`, `~/Downloads/PDMX-mxl`; method in `~/Downloads/PDMX.md`), accepting a bar when two scores agree or one matches at 0.95+ (`%%bar N one-score`: lighter italic numbers and a "bar numbers from one score" chip), hand-checked bars in `theme-sources/pdmx-checked.json` winning, into `theme-sources/pdmx-matches.json`; `convert-mtd.py` writes it as `%%bar N`, and the cards number each bar from there (all numbers of a line level, above anything in their way; font: Commit Mono's digits with a plain zero, `python3 scripts/make-barno-font.py`). Play plays grace notes on the beat and ornament signs played out (`apps/shared/ornament-play.js`, `OrnamentPlay`: trill, prall, mordent, turn; before 1828 / Bach..Beethoven from the note above, later from the main note, as the lesson's tables; also used by `AbcPlay`); the lesson on how to play them was `lessons/grace-notes-ornaments.qmd`, **delisted 2026-10-20 as a work in progress** (out of the sidebar, the Lessons listing, search and the tool catalogue via `search: false`; its URL still works; open items in a comment at its top) and replaced in the menus by `lessons/ornaments-by-period.qmd` (the five idioms on one small note and one trill, valid / not appropriate readings per period, improvisation then and now, and a flow chart, `apps/shared/lessons/ornament-flow.js`, `OrnamentFlow`, composers -> answers; test `node tests/lessons/ornaments-by-period.cdp.js` after `sh scripts/render-test-page.sh lessons/ornaments-by-period.qmd`). The old page (sources `scripts/sources/ornaments.py`; its ornament tables are data in `scripts/ornaments/*.py`, written into the page by `python3 scripts/ornament-tables.py`; each sign is drawn by abcjs and plays its written-out version, `abc-ex.js` `data-play`; a row's `bend` plays through the band's clean guitar with real slides, `abc-ex.js` `data-bend` -> `BandSounds` `spec.bend`, test `node tests/lessons/ornament-bends.cdp.js`; LilyPond source for engraved SVGs in `scripts/ornaments/lily/`; `python3 scripts/ornament-lily.py` writes every drawn example as LilyPond there, one file each and `all-examples.ly`). The whole old set is on an unlisted reference page,
  `tools/classical-themes-rough.qmd` (same page script): `node scripts/clean-rough-themes.mjs` writes
  `scripts/theme-sources/rough/` (MTD's version where it has the theme, `%%from mtd`; else the old midi2abc ABC with beams
  by the site's rules (notation-doc.js `beam()`) and repeated accidentals dropped, each checked note for note against the
  original with AbcTune and abcjs; a theme sitting very high moved down an octave by a rule fitted on MTD's themes, `%%repaired
  octave`; tied-apart triplets not repaired: their barlines are wrong too), then `node scripts/import-themes.mjs --rough` -> `apps/themes/themes-rough-data.js`. Test: `node tests/lessons/classical-themes.cdp.js` (after rendering it).

  the site password). `node scripts/mtd-recordings.mjs` writes `apps/themes/themes-recordings.js` (loaded on Classical Themes
  only, between works-data.js and works.js): added to a theme's work if it has one, else a work `mtd-<id>` of its own. MP3s
  (128k) in `~/Downloads/mtd-mp3/`; upload with `cf r2 buckets objects upload mtd/<id>.mp3 --bucket-name everything-music-mlt
  --file <id>.mp3 --content-type audio/mpeg`.
- Roman numerals (optional, "Show Roman numerals" on the page, off by default; "Only themes that have them" filters): `node scripts/theme-romantext.mjs [--report]`
  matches each theme to a When in Rome RomanText analysis (composer + Op./K./BWV + movement from the corpus folders and the theme's title; ids
  and rules in its header), puts the theme's bars on the movement's bars from `t.bar` (+ pickup) and keeps the match only if the notes fit the
  chords (a theme with a sourced bar needs fit >= 0.6; one with no bar, bar 1 assumed, must also beat every other starting bar by 0.05, so
  few pass: **78 of 2,067 so far**, almost all Beethoven / Haydn / Mozart / Bach). Writes `themes-rn.js` (`window.THEME_RN`, id -> body length
  + [char offset in the theme's body, label]), drawn as `"_label"` annotations by `withRn` in themes-page.js. Rerun after themes-data.js
  changes (an entry whose body length no longer matches is ignored). More themes would need their bar: a PDMX match, or matching against the
  analysed scores' ABC. Test in `tests/lessons/classical-themes.cdp.js`.

- Pop Themes (`tools/pop-themes.qmd`, same `themes-page.js`, which switches on `window.POP_THEMES`): **three collections, 2,411 sections** (CoCoPops and RS200: next bullet); BiMMuDa's 1,104 section melodies (verse, chorus, bridge…), the Billboard year-end top-5 singles 1950-2022, main melody only (the repository shows no licence: **licence unverified**, so it stays behind the site password; credited on the page and in `scripts/sources/pop-themes.py`; **no lyrics**, `bimmuda_lyrics/` is never used). `python3 -I scripts/convert-bimmuda.py` (needs `pip install mido`; dataset in ~/Downloads/BiMMuDa/BiMMuDa) reads each section's MIDI itself (the sections exist only as MIDI; MuseScore / MusicXML would add no spelling) and writes `scripts/theme-sources/bimmuda/<ID>.abc`: grid of sixteenths and triplets, lengths from the sounding length / 0.95, key = the CSV's Tonic + Mode (C# / G# major as Db / Ab, Gb / Db minor as F# / C#), Q from the MIDI tempo, pickups, beams and off-beat cuts by the notation page's rules (`L:1/8`). `EXPECT_OUT=f.json python3 -I scripts/convert-bimmuda.py` then `node scripts/check-bimmuda.mjs f.json` checks every file against its MIDI with abcjs and AbcTune (pitch and onset of every note). Then `node scripts/import-themes.mjs --set pop` (`--set bimmuda` is the same) -> `apps/themes/pop-themes-data.js` (`window.POP_THEMES`, all three collections, `set` on each; the CSV's BPM, tonality, MIC, pitch STD, MIS, onset density, nPVI, RIC as sort values, the song's Spotify link, genres, chords; the classical output is unchanged). Page: filters are the finder plus selects for section / decade / genre / chart position (they combine), the dataset's figures as sorts, "Show chords" in place of Roman numerals. Genres: `python3 -I scripts/bimmuda-genres.py` (MusicBrainz recording + artist tags, Wikidata P136 song + artist; cached in `theme-sources/bimmuda-genres-cache-*.json`, generic User-Agent, MusicBrainz takes about 40 minutes cold; Wikidata is slow and throttled, so it is asked only about songs MusicBrainz's tags place nowhere, and added almost nothing) -> `theme-sources/bimmuda-genres.json`, mapped to pop / rock / r&b/soul / country / hip-hop / jazz/standards / dance/disco / folk, top one or two; `theme-sources/bimmuda-genre-fixes.json` (`{"1958_03a": ["pop"]}`, keyed year_position) wins (22 hand fixes in it from general knowledge: the 19 songs no source placed and three mis-tags). `--merge` rebuilds `bimmuda-genres.json` from the caches without requests. Chords: `node scripts/bimmuda-salami.mjs [--report]` matches songs to the McGill Billboard set by title + an artist word (23 in both), takes the same-label sections within one bar of the melody's length, transposes to BiMMuDa's tonic, scores on-beat notes against the chord tones at bar offsets -2..+2 and keeps a section only at fit >= 0.6 and 0.05 above the next different answer (17 of 62 sections of those songs) -> `theme-sources/bimmuda-chords.json` -> `chords` in the data. Tests `RAW=1 node tests/lessons/pop-themes.cdp.js` and `RAW=1 node tests/lessons/classical-themes.cdp.js` (`RAW=1`: the page from its .qmd's HTML block, `tests/lessons/qmd-raw-page.js`, because Quarto 1.10 cannot run on macOS 13; with Quarto: `sh scripts/render-test-page.sh tools/pop-themes.qmd` first). Not done: `research_notes/bimmuda-plan.md`.
- Pop Themes, CoCoPops (CC BY-NC 4.0) and RS200 (CC BY 4.0, taken from CoCoPops' Humdrum copy of it: sections, absolute chords, spelled pitches; the original v2.1 `.mel` only as a cross-check): `python3 -I scripts/convert-cocopops.py [billboard|rolling-stone|both]` (corpora in `~/Projects/corpora/cocopops`; reads the main vocal line, never the lyrics) -> `scripts/theme-sources/cocopops/cc_<McGill id>_<n>.abc` (793 sections, 214 songs) and `rs200/rs_<rank>_<n>.abc` (514, 194 songs), `cocopops-songs.json` / `rs200-songs.json`. One ABC per distinct section (label + letter, first occurrence with a melody; repeats counted in `%%repeats`), pickups moved across the section boundary, real spelling and the source's key (a dorian / mixolydian tonic: the relative major's signature + `%%mode`; an RS200 tonic with no mode and a mostly minor tonic chord: minor, `%%keyfrom chords`), chords from `**harte` written as ABC chord symbols at the exact change (a note is split with a tie to put one there; the importer moves them into the `chords` side list). Billboard has real durations; **RS200 has onsets only**, so a note lasts to the next onset (to the barline before an empty bar): lengths approximate, the card says so. Tempo `Q:` from the source's timestamps. Checks: `EXPECT_OUT=f.json COUNTS_OUT=c.json python3 -I scripts/convert-cocopops.py both`, then `node scripts/check-cocopops.mjs f.json` (abcjs without warnings, bars add up, pitch + onset of every note, every chord at its exact time) and `python3 -I scripts/check-cocopops-source.py c.json` (note counts re-counted from the raw files; RS200 `.mel` onsets against the Humdrum). Genres for all three: `scripts/bimmuda-genres.py` (cache keys `cc_<id>`, `rs_<rank>`; a song already cached under another key reuses its answer). The importer groups a song found in more than one collection (title, an artist word, years within 2: 44 songs): `also` / `grp` (the card's "Also in…", `?song=<grp>`), and gives every song with a McGill id a "Billboard chords" link to Timestamp Notes `?salami=<id>` (CoCoPops' own; BiMMuDa's through `bimmuda-chords.json` or a CoCoPops twin). Page: Collection select, BiMMuDa's figures sort last-for-the-others, section list extended. Open: `research_notes/bimmuda-plan.md`.
