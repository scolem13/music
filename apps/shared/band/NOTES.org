* Added in the second session
** Guitar voice-led triads
Two guitar voicing styles in voicings.js, both three notes on ONE string set, each chord taking the
shape whose strings move least from the one before (ChordVoicings.lead):
- =triadvl= root-3rd-5th of every chord (a 7th chord gives its plain triad).
- =uppervl= the triad on the 3rd of a 7th chord (3-5-7), 6-R-3 for a 6th chord, b7-9-4 for a 7sus.
The band plays them on strings 4-3-2 up to fret 12 (GUITAR_SETS in harmony.js). The Chord Sheet has a
menu item per string set; a shape picked by hand is kept and the chords after it follow.
Known: through a cycle of fourths (ii-V-I-VI) the nearest shape is always the next one UP, so the
line climbs the neck until it hits fret 12 and jumps back down. That is true voice leading, but it
may not be what is wanted; an alternative is to keep every chord inside one five-fret position.
** Meters
- A beat is the meter's lower note, except in 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8 (compound), where it is the dotted
  quarter: 6/8 has 2 beats, 12/8 has 4, and the tempo counts dotted quarters. BandHarmony.meterInfo
  decides; form bars, ctx and bar records carry =meter= and =compound=.
- In a compound bar x.5 is the beat's THIRD eighth (the player warps it to 2/3); x.333 needs straight:true.
- 3/4: jazz waltz (ride ding, ding-a, ding; hat foot on 2 and 3; kick on 1), bass walks three quarters.
- 6/8, 12/8: every eighth on the cymbal, kick on 1 (and 3), backbeat on 2 (and 4), whatever Feel says
  (the Feel menu is disabled). Bass walks one note per beat with pickup eighths; the riff is a shuffle.
  Comping uses the same figures as 4/4, which land on the triplet grid.
- The page has a Meter menu that re-writes the three blues presets; custom ABC keeps its own M: line.
- Notation writes compound bars in eighths (M:12/8, Q:3/8=); MIDI writes the time signature and
  converts the tempo to quarters.
- NOT done: 5/4, 7/4 and other meters still get the generic four-beat patterns cut to length; 2/2 is
  counted as two half-note beats; no 12/8-specific comping figure (repeated triplet chords); a tune
  that changes between simple and compound meter keeps one tempo number for both kinds of beat.

* Guitar: led by the hand, and two-note chords (2026-10-07, later)

  notes on awkward fingerings and hopped string sets. Now ChordVoicings.easiest picks the smallest move
  for the fretting hand: frets the hand shifts + half the fret change on each shared string + 0.75 per
  string picked up or dropped (handMotion); pitch motion only breaks near-ties, with a mild pull to fret 5.
  Used by the band for every guitar style and by the Chord Sheet's "Favor voice leading" on all fretted
  instruments. The weights are first guesses.
