# DAW lessons

- The DAW lessons (`lessons/daw-*.qmd`, eight, in `_quarto.yml` after the Recording lessons; include `lessons/_daw-kit.qmd`):
  a small DAW, `apps/shared/daw/mini-daw.js` (`MiniDaw`; `<div class="mdaw" data-id data-preset data-show>`, documented at its top),
  playing the band's sounds (`BandSounds`) with its parts written by `BandMidi.generate` ("Session players"). Timeline, loop, click,
  step sequencer, piano roll, record from computer keys / MIDI, audio tracks from the mic (DAW 6: bleed, headphones, latency), quantize, clips, mixer, MIDI export, bounce to WAV, undo.
  Each lesson ends with an "In other DAWs" table (BandLab, Ableton Live, Logic Pro); DAW 8 (`daw-real.qmd`, no DAW on it) is a tabbed
  first-session checklist for each, every step linked to its manual page. Checked Oct 2026 against BandLab Help Center, Live 12 manual, Logic 12.3 guide. Test: `node tests/lessons/daw.cdp.js` (after rendering them).
- MIDI in: **Open MIDI** reads any .mid (`MiniDaw.fromMidi`: a track per file track + channel, channel 10 to kit pieces or congas, GM program to the nearest instrument; quarter-note time; the file's time signatures (meta 0x58) become the project's meter and meter changes, one mid-bar moves to the next bar line; first tempo only; at most 64 bars, 12 tracks; what was changed is said in the status). The Backing Track's Export MIDI → **Open in the DAW** leaves the file in localStorage `mdaw:handoff` and opens `lessons/daw-export.html`, where the first DAW with Files takes it (within 10 min; replaces the song, Undo brings it back). Test `node tests/lessons/daw-midi.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail); the hand-off is in daw.cdp.js (render `tools/backing-track.qmd` too).
- Time signatures: project `ts` [n,d] + `tsMap` [{bar,ts}] (changes from a bar on; missing = 4/4), all bar maths in `MiniDaw.Meter` (mini-daw.js, "time signatures"): time stays in quarter notes, a bar is n*4/d beats, clicks fall on groups (quarters in x/4, dotted quarters in 6/8 9/8 12/8, 2+2+3 in 7/8, 3+2 in 5/8). Transport "Time ... from bar" menu (2/4 3/4 4/4 5/4 3/8 5/8 6/8 7/8 9/8 12/8), `data-ts="6/8"` starts a DAW in a meter. Ruler, loop, clip snap and lengths in bars, grid lines, position display, count-in (one bar), record length, MIDI export (0x58 per change) and import follow it; the band's Session players write in the meter at the playhead (its 5/8 and 7/8 are 5 or 7 beats squeezed to eighths). Tempo is quarter notes a minute in every meter; the preset drum / bass patterns stay 4/4 patterns; clips keep their beat place when the meter changes. Tests `node tests/lessons/daw-meter.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail), meter block in `tests/lessons/daw.cdp.js` (`SITE=<dir> PORT=<n>` for a scratch render; its mic-recording check fails offline, as before).
- Session view (2026-11-10; `apps/shared/daw/session.js`, `data-show="session"`, lesson `lessons/daw-session.qmd` "DAW 4b", presets `lionloop` / `lion`): a pared-down Ableton Session View / Logic Live Loops on the same tracks and mixer. `p.scenes` + `track.slots` (slot clips loop; one a track; scene ▶ launches the row and an empty slot stops its track; launch waits for the next bar or beat; its own clock, stopped by playing the arrangement). Arrangement → Session: **Loop → scene** copies the loop bracket's notes into a new scene. Session → Arrangement: **● Record to Arrangement** logs launches from a bar line to a bar line and writes looped clips cut at the bar lines (replacing those tracks' clips in the span; turns the loop off). Chord roots: each scene row shows its roots (lowest note starting each bar of the bass slot) with their place in the key; clicking one cycles I-IV-V and moves every pitched clip in that bar by the same interval (parallel, right for major chords only); **Key** moves all pitched notes. Header of session.js has the model. The Lion Sleeps Tonight progression I-IV-I-V in F is as commonly taught, unchecked against the record; tempo and parts are ours. Not done: audio clips in the grid, dragging clips between slots, follow actions, a third (minor / ii, vi) chord choice, MIDI export of scenes. Facts about Live / Logic checked from search summaries only (sources in `scripts/sources/voice-daw-dj.py`). Test `node tests/lessons/daw-session.cdp.js` (render the lesson first; serves the samples from tests/band/lead/site/sf).
- Keyboard clip placement (2026-11-10; `Daw.prototype.arrKey` in mini-daw.js, table in daw-arrange.qmd): arrows move the playhead / track, [ ] pick clips, N new clip, P places the last-selected Session clip (P P P repeats), Shift+arrows move the clip, - = resize it, Alt = beats. Arrangement view only. Found while testing: the constructor's `this.key` (storage name) had been shadowing `Daw.prototype.key`, so NO DAW shortcut (Space, L, C, R, Q, undo) worked; the field is now `storeKey`.
- Volume automation, the foundation (2026-11-10; `data-show="automation"`, section "volume automation" in mini-daw.js; preview in daw-mixer.qmd): `track.auto = { vol: [{ t beats, v dB }] }`. The points ARE the level (the fader locks, "(A)" in the mixer); held before the first and after the last point, straight in dB between. Each strip has an `auto` gain node (input → auto → pan → fader): scheduled a step ahead in `schedule()` (reset on loop jumps), set to the playhead's level when stopped (`applyMix`), written offline in the bounce. Keys: V lane, A point at the playhead (at the level it already has), ↑↓ ±1 dB, ←→ ±1 beat (Shift 6 dB / a bar, Alt fine), [ ] points, Delete, Esc; mouse click / drag / double-click. Export: MIDI CC 7 per track (GM2 curve `dB = 40 log10(cc/127)`, so 127 = 0 dB is the ceiling and boosts above 0 dB export as 127; a change at most every sixteenth, so ramps are stepped ~2 dB on steep falls), read back by `fromMidi` (a constant CC 7 sets the fader; a varying one becomes points thinned to 0.5 dB). NOT verified: what Live and Logic do with CC 7 on import (search summaries could not confirm it; the lesson says to look). Only volume, only per track, absolute (not relative to the fader). Next steps if wanted: pan (CC 10) and other CCs as the same `auto.<name>` shape, a parameter list in the lane, clip-level (Live clip-envelope style) lanes, curves, copying points with clips. Tests `node tests/lessons/daw-automation.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail), `tests/lessons/daw-automation.cdp.js`.
- Vim keys (2026-11-10): h j k l = ← ↓ ↑ → (H J K L = with Shift; read from `e.code`, so Alt works on a Mac), and a number before a key repeats it ("3k" = +3 dB on an automation point, "3l" three bars, "3p" three copies; `Daw.count` / `this.n` in `key()`; a lone 0 is not a count). Loop moved from L to **O**. Velocity vs. volume automation is explained in daw-mixer.qmd (this DAW's level = velocity^1.6 from `sounds.js`; piano / e-pianos also filter soft notes darker); a keyboard velocity flow is proposed there but NOT built. Microtonality (31-EDO): not built; the sampler already repitches fractional MIDI numbers, but the roll, keys, export and band are 12-TET (see the notes in the conversation / ask).
- Note mode, step 1 (2026-11-10; `edKey` / `vimModel` / `vimApply` in mini-daw.js, plan in `TODO.md`, test `tests/lessons/daw-notemode.cdp.js`): Enter on a selected clip, Esc leaves; motions h j k l w b e ( ) { } 0 $, objects iw ic ih ib ip iv, operators > < gU gu (doubled = this chord), r1-r8, counts (before the operator = how much, after = how far), `.`, u / Ctrl-r. A chord = notes sharing a start; a beat = a quarter note. Enter no longer means "back to the start" when a clip is selected (Home does). The band's session players' events all carry `vel` (checked 2026-11-10 across every style), but a chord's notes share one velocity and the patterns add random humanising, so two runs differ. Lesson `daw-notation.qmd` (DAW 3b, preset `ode`): the DAW vs written notation, what a computer must be told vs what performers infer.
- Feel (2026-11-10; `applyFeel`, `humanize`, the Feel menu and Humanize clip button in the Session players row; `data-feel="band|loose"` starts a DAW on it; tests `node tests/lessons/daw-feel.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail), `tests/lessons/daw-feel.cdp.js`): writes the live band's human touches into clips (numbers read from `BandPlayer.FEEL`, i.e. `LAY` / `JITTER` / `STRUM` / `VEL_JITTER` in player.js): lay-back per part, triangle jitter, chord roll low to high (strums 28 ms, `strum` flag carried from the band's events), velocity wander. NOT tempo: the band's tempo is constant and so is the DAW's (no rubato anywhere). `clip.felt` marks a clip that has it (Humanize refuses twice; Quantize at 100% with no note selection clears the mark). The Backing Track's MIDI export does not add the touches either. Default stays Tight so the other lessons are unchanged.
