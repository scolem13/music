# Theory Drills

- Theory Drills (`theory/theory-drills.qmd`, `apps/shared/drills/drills.js` + `.css`, `window.Drills`; Theory sidebar): note names,
  intervals, key signatures, triads (`triad`: lead-sheet symbol typed, case-sensitive: A major, a / Am minor; `spell`: write or correct the
  notes). `theory/triads.qmd` follows Hutchinson's ch. 6 (the source of truth), with musictheory.net lessons / embedded exercises and Open Music
  Theory links; text written for the site (the book is GFDL). `theory/roman-numerals.qmd` (ch. 7): `rn` (numerals, case = quality, /3rd /5th,
  also 6 / 64), `rnwrite`, `cadence` (AC PC DC HC; minor = the book's common set i ii° III iv V VI VII vii°, `minorset=all` adds the rare five);
  the book's YouTube clips with titles checked by YouTube oEmbed. `theory/seventh-chords.qmd` (ch. 8): `seventh` (maj7 7 m7 ø7 °7, every label in the book's
  Figure 8.1.2; a small root = minor, c7 = Cm7), `spell7`, `rn7` (IM7 ii7 … viiø7; minor i7 iiø7 IIIM7 iv7 V7 VIM7 vii°7; /3rd /5th /7th or 65 43 42),
  `rn7write`. `theory/progressions.qmd` (ch. 9): `func` (T / TP / PD / D from the book's flowcharts) and `prog` (a progression from `PROGS`,
  shown and/or played, typed as numerals or symbols; `chordIn` takes a lowered degree as `-d`, for ♭VII). `theory/non-chord-tones.qmd` (ch. 10): `nct` (two bars of melody over three chords, the non-chord tone marked *; `NCT_FIG` builds each type, `nctMelody` searches for a melody where every other note is a chord tone; feedback via `explain`: accented, approach and departure, suspension figure) and `nctrule` (the book's Table 10.1.1 reversed). Generated questions are checked by `abc-nht.js`: `node tests/lessons/nct-drill.test.js`. `theory/figured-bass.qmd` (ch. 16): `figured.js` (load after drills.js): `fbrn` / `fbwrite` (wrap `rn` / `rn7` / `rnwrite` / `rn7write`, answers as the book's figures I64 V65 IM43 viiø42, an inverted seventh drops the 7; slash forms accepted), `fbfig` (the chord on the staff → which figure, buttons), `fbreal` (bass + figures → the notes, key signature only), `sixfour` (cadential / passing / pedal in four voices, the site's own voicings); exercise builder ch. 16 uses fbrn fbwrite fbreal. Its inversion explorer is `fb-explorer.js` (`<div class="fb-explorer" data-key data-mode data-chord data-seventh data-inv>`, abcjs draws the grand staff, then noteheads are coloured by chord member and the brackets and figures added). Tests: `node tests/lessons/figured-drill.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail), `node tests/lessons/figured-bass.cdp.js` (after rendering the page). `theory/secondary-dominants.qmd` (ch. 17): `secdom` (name it on the staff, followed by its resolution; book figures V6 V64 V65 V43 V42, `normSec`), `secwrite`, `secsym` (Roman numeral ↔ chord symbol); `secMake` leaves out chords made only of the key's notes (V/IV major, V/III V/VI V7/III minor). Test: `node tests/lessons/secdom-drill.test.js`. `theory/secondary-diminished.qmd` (ch. 18): `secdim`, `secdimwrite`, `secdimsym` (vii°/x, vii°7/x before a minor chord, viiø7/x before a major one, as in the book's Figures 18.2.1–2; root a half step below the target, `normSecDim`); `secDimMake` leaves out chords made only of the key's notes (vii°/III, viiø7/III in minor). Test: `node tests/lessons/secdim-drill.test.js`. `theory/mode-mixture.qmd` (ch. 19): engines in their own file, `mixture.js` (load after drills.js): `mix` (name the borrowed chord on the staff; the book's Figures 19.2.1–2: i ii° iiø7 ♭III iv v ♭VI ♭VII ♭VII7 vii°7, plus iv7, major keys; figures 6 64 65 43 42, `normMix`), `mixwrite`, `mixsym` (numeral ↔ symbol), `mixdc` (I IV/ii V then vi or ♭VI, by ear) and `picardy` (minor phrase ending i or I). Phrases write an accidental for any letter already altered in the bar. Test: `node tests/lessons/mixture-drill.test.js` (`MUTATE=1` is a negative control that must fail). `theory/neapolitan.qmd` (ch. 20): engines in their own file, `neapolitan.js` (load after drills.js): `neap` (N / N6 on the staff followed by V, typed as the book's N, ♭II accepted, `normN`; `chords=pd` mixes in iv iv6 ii°6 / IV IV6 ii ii6), `neapwrite`, `neapsym` (numeral ↔ symbol, D♭/F for N6 in C), `neapear` (i – N6 / iv / ii°6 – V – i, four voices, only the top note differs; the voicing is the site's own). Test: `node tests/lessons/neapolitan-drill.test.js` (`NEG=1` must fail). `theory/augmented-sixths.qmd` (ch. 21): `aug6` (name It / Fr / Ger / EnGer on the staff, `V7N` optional: the same sound spelled A♭ C E♭ G♭ in C), `aug6write` (bass ♭6 first; It+6 three notes, a doubled tonic accepted), `aug6sym` (the book's symbols: 7 on ♭6, It no 5th, Fr ♭5; back to the name, Ger or EnGer both right before a major I64), `aug6ear` (I – ? – I64 – V – I with iv6, N6, It, Fr, Ger; fixed four-part voicings in C moved to the key). Test: `node tests/lessons/aug6-drill.test.js`. `theory/modulation.qmd` (ch. 22): no drills engine; `apps/shared/modulation.js` (`window.Modulation`: numerals spelled in any key, `"vi|G: ii"` = a pivot, `"I||"` = phrase end; voiced bass + 3 upper parts) drives `.mod-related`, `.mod-pivot` (common chords of two keys), `.mod-ex data-kind` (EXAMPLES) and `.mod-quiz`; links into Analysed Scores' Moonlight (`?piece=…&bars=a-b`, the key strip and form table in `analysed-scores.js`). Sections fold via `apps/shared/section-fold.js`. Test: `node tests/lessons/modulation.cdp.js`. `theory/jazz-theory.qmd` (ch. 31): `jazz.js` (load after drills.js), `jzwrite` / `jzname` (the book's Figures 31.4.1–8 on 12 roots; `Drills.parseJazz` reads a symbol by the book's rules, 31.1–31.2: every number up to the highest, 9(13) adds only the 13, sus replaces the 3rd and 11; a name fits when it means the same notes over the same root, a missing 5th allowed unless a 5th or ♯5 is drawn; written notes checked by sound over the correctly spelled root), `jzguide`, `jztt`, `jz251`, `jzear` (close voicings), `jzscale` / `jzscalewrite` (Table 31.9.4), `jzchordscale` (Table 31.10.3); band demos and a tritone-sub `.ar-quiz`. Test: `node tests/lessons/jazz-drill.test.js` (tables by hand from the figures). `theory/melodic-analysis.qmd` (ch. 11): `motive` lives in its own file, `motives.js` (load after drills.js; adds `Drills.engines.motive`): a motive in bar 1, one of the book's alterations (or a plain sequence) in bar 2; `fits` drops any question more than one answer fits. Test: `node tests/lessons/motive-drill.test.js`. `theory/pop-forms.qmd` (ch. 12) and `theory/accompaniment-textures.qmd` (ch. 14) have no drills: band demos and `.ar-quiz` from `lessons/_arrange-kit.qmd` (a choice may carry its own `chart` / `charts` and `sections`); test both with `node tests/lessons/textures.cdp.js`. An exercise sets `midiNeed` (notes before a MIDI chord is judged). Guitar chords through a DI (`GuitarIn`): plan only, `scripts/guitar-di/GUITAR-IN.md`. Settings come from the address (`?ex=interval&ints=m3,M3&clef=bass&count=20&lock=1`, names as in each
  exercise's SETTINGS); a student's changes are kept per link (localStorage `drills-v1:<ex>:<link settings>`); answers by button, keys or
  MIDI (pitch only). A lesson can embed one: `<div class="drill" data-ex="note" data-settings="...">`. Test: `node tests/lessons/drills.cdp.js`
  (after rendering it; checks generated questions against abcjs's reading of the drawn ABC).
